Frequently Asked Questions

Real Farm Simulator FAQ

Answers to common questions about Real Farm Simulator, development and availability.

What is Real Farm Simulator?

Real Farm Simulator is a mobile farming simulation developed by Silicon Games. It combines agriculture, machinery, livestock and farm management.

Where can I play the game?

The game is being distributed for Android. The current availability and version can be checked through the official Google Play listing on this website.

Is the game still being developed?

Yes. Real Farm Simulator is an independently developed project, and its systems and content can continue to evolve through future updates.

What can I do in the game?

Players can work with crops, agricultural machinery, livestock and farm management systems while developing their own farming operation.

Will new features be added?

Future updates may introduce improvements, new systems and additional content as development progresses.
